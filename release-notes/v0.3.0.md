**Idle variety on the ground, and ladders.**

**Idle variety:** a crew member standing still now plays one of their character's idle variants every 10 to 20 seconds, then goes back to the normal idle. Moving cancels it. The game already does this for seated crew and on the jetpack, but not on the ground.

- With [KSP Characters](https://github.com/paulo-santana/KSA-KSP-Characters) 0.6.0 or later, the Kerbals look around, stretch and fidget with KSP's own idle animations.

**Ladders:** a character can now hold a pose while still on a ladder and climb down with its own clip. The game has one climbing clip for both directions, and it freezes that clip whenever the position doesn't change, which is most frames, so its climb hardly played. With [KSP Characters](https://github.com/paulo-santana/KSA-KSP-Characters) 0.6.0, the Kerbals use KSP's ladder hold and climb-down animations and climb smoothly.

For mod authors: list the variants in your asset XML as `<CharacterManager.AnimIdleAction Id="..."/>` inside a character's `CharacterGroundAnimations`, and the ladder clips as `<CharacterManager.AnimLadderIdle Id="..."/>` and `<CharacterManager.AnimLadderDown Id="..."/>`. The game skips those lines, so the mod still loads without Character Manager. See the README.
