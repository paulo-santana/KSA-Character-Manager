**The RCS stance, and jetpacks of the character's own. For KSA 2026.11.10.5801.**

- **Works on KSA 2026.11.10.5801 and later,** through StarMap 0.5.2 or later. 0.3.0 found no characters on that build, and StarMap 0.4.x can't start it at all. (0.4.0 was never released.)

**RCS stance:** while the jetpack's RCS is on, a crew member on the ground can stand, walk and jump with their own clips, and turning RCS on or off while standing plays a toggle animation. As in KSP, moving cancels the toggle animation; the stance itself still switches.

**Jetpack arms:** the game has one clip for the jetpack's arms and plays it backwards when RCS comes on. A character can now add a clip for the arms coming out, played forward in step with the body.

**RCS puffs:** a character with its own jetpack can put the thruster puffs at that jetpack's thrusters. Only the visuals move; the thrust stays the game's, so the jetpack flies exactly as before. To compare, untick "RCS puffs from the character's own jetpack" under Jetpacks in View > Mods > Characters.

- With [KSP Characters](https://github.com/paulo-santana/KSA-KSP-Characters) 0.7.1 or later, the Kerbals get all of this from KSP's own jetpack and animations.
- After a game update, Character Manager checks the parts of the game it relies on. If something has changed, it turns off only the features that depend on it and logs an error, instead of breaking the game.

**For mod authors: the extensions moved to their own file.** Since 5801 the game drops a whole asset that holds a field it doesn't know, so the `CharacterManager.` tags can't stay in your asset XML anymore.
- They go in a `CharacterManager.xml` next to your `mod.toml`. Don't list it in `assets`.
- It's written the way the game patches an asset: each block names your asset by `Id` with `Modify="Patch"`, and lists sit in containers.
- The idle variants became one list, `CharacterManager.AnimIdleActions`.
- New fields: `CharacterManager.Anim<slot>Rcs`, `AnimRcsOn` and `AnimRcsOff` in `CharacterGroundAnimations`; `CharacterManager.MMUArmExtendAnim` in `CharacterMMUAnimations`; and `CharacterManager.RcsPuffs` in the jetpack's `CharacterAttachment`.
