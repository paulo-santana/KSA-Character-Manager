**Idle variety on the ground:** a crew member standing still now plays one of their character's idle variants every 10 to 20 seconds, then goes back to the normal idle. Moving cancels it. The game already does this for seated crew and on the jetpack, but not on the ground.

- With [KSP Characters](https://github.com/paulo-santana/KSA-KSP-Characters) 0.6.0 or later, the Kerbals look around, stretch and fidget with KSP's own idle animations.
- For mod authors: list the variants in your asset XML as `<CharacterManager.AnimIdleAction Id="..."/>` inside a character's `CharacterGroundAnimations`. The game skips that line, so the mod still loads without Character Manager. See the README.
